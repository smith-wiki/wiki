---
title: ConvAI Innovations
summary: Developer of the open-weight Laya typed-decision model family.
---

ConvAI Innovations develops [Laya](../laya/), a family of publicly released typed-decision models with an Apache-2.0 Python runtime and downloadable checkpoints. Its founder and CEO Nandakishor Mukkunnoth introduces the project on the company's Laya research site. The upstream code and model card identify ConvAI as the organization behind the release. ([Mukkunnoth, n.d.](../../sources/laya-research-announcement/); [ConvAI Innovations and Laya contributors, n.d.](../../sources/laya-repository/); [ConvAI Innovations, n.d.](../../sources/laya-model-card/))

Its dashboard privacy notice names the legal operator as Convai Innovations Pvt. Ltd. in Kerala, India; this identifies the organization, not Laya's architecture. ([Convai Innovations Pvt. Ltd., 2026](../../sources/convai-privacy-policy/))

This is a **different vendor** from [TypeSafe AI](../typesafe-ai/), which develops [Jev](../jev/). The two products expose similar typed-decision interfaces, but public evidence does not establish a shared company or a shared model implementation. See [A shared decision API does not reveal a shared model](../../notes/a-shared-decision-api-does-not-reveal-a-shared-model/). ([Mukkunnoth, n.d.](../../sources/laya-research-announcement/); [Almeida, 2026](../../sources/typesafe-system-one-jev/); [TypeSafe AI, n.d.](../../sources/typesafe-system-one-documentation/))
