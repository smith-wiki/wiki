title: Scientific PDF structure extraction
summary: Parser choice depends on formula, table, heading, code, and caption contracts, not a single PDF-to-Markdown score.
---

## What it means

A scientific PDF is a rendered page sequence, not a stored Markdown document. Conversion must recover reading order, section hierarchy, tables, equations, code, and the relation of figures to captions. Markdown serializes some of these but can flatten geometry and associations; keep the original PDF and, where available, ordered block/JSON/TEI output alongside the Markdown reading copy. This extends [web capture fidelity](../web-capture-fidelity/), while the [benchmark distinction](../../notes/pdf-benchmark-scores-do-not-certify-structure/) explains why a high aggregate cannot certify every element ([Docling project, n.d.](../../sources/docling-repository/); [Ouyang et al., 2025](../../sources/omnidocbench-study/)).

For [visual evidence retrieval](../visual-evidence-retrieval/), keep figure/caption boxes in the original PDF coordinate system and link each verified crop back to its source page. A page-image match can find the right page without locating the diagram or proving its caption; [page relevance is not figure provenance](../../notes/page-retrieval-does-not-localize-a-figure/) ([GROBID project, n.d.](../../sources/grobid-pdf-coordinates/); [Loison et al., 2026](../../sources/vidore-three-study/)).

## What the published comparisons actually show

All numbers below are **reported results, not tests run for this turn**. olmOCR-Bench scores are percent unit tests passed (higher is better) on selected text, pairwise order, table relationships, and rendered formulas; no heading-tree, code-fence, or caption-link score. The olmOCR 2 study reports **olmOCR 2 82.4 +/- 1.1**, **Marker 1.10.1 76.1 +/- 1.1**, **MinerU 2.5.4 75.2 +/- 1.1** (author-reported), and **Mistral OCR API 72.0 +/- 1.1**. The evaluator's authors also developed olmOCR, the Mistral API checkpoint is not identified, and these numbers do not describe MinerU 4 or current Mistral OCR 4.1 ([Poznanski et al., 2025](../../sources/olmocr-two-study/); [Allen Institute for AI, n.d.](../../sources/olmocr-bench-dataset/)).

OmniDocBench **v1.6_full**, a different 1,651-page corpus/evaluator, reports overall as the mean of text accuracy, formula CDM, and table TEDS; **reading-order error is excluded**. Lower order error is better. It lists **MinerU-2.5 VLM 93.04** overall (order 0.130), **MinerU-Pipeline 3.4.0 86.47** (0.153), **olmOCR 85.74** (0.216; checkpoint not identified), **Mistral OCR 2503 85.66** (0.171), and **Marker 1.8.2 78.44** (0.243). Docling is mentioned in model information but has no v1.6 score. Neither v1.0 nor v1.5 can be numerically mixed with v1.6: corpus, matching, and overall definition changed ([OpenDataLab, n.d.](../../sources/omnidocbench-repository/); [Ouyang et al., 2025](../../sources/omnidocbench-study/)).

For whole scientific papers, **READoc-arXiv** tests 1,009 multi-page documents and scores headings, embedded/isolated formulas, tables, and order separately. In its historical, unpinned parser snapshot, MinerU averages **73.07**, Marker **64.98**, Docling **58.83**, and PyMuPDF4LLM **40.55** (higher is better). MinerU's heading-tree score is **41.97** and formula scores **62.77/70.76**; Marker reaches table-tree **72.36** but embedded-formula **3.47**; Docling's formula scores **0.23/0.00**. ArXiv ground truth comes from successfully converted LaTeX sources, not manual PDF transcription; these old configurations are not current-version rankings ([Li et al., 2025](../../sources/readoc-study/)).

**Inference:** For scientific PDFs where math and tables must survive, **MinerU is the best-supported quality candidate among the named parsers in the published multi-page comparison**, and its 2.5 VLM leads the named OmniDocBench v1.6 rows. This is *not* a measured claim about MinerU 4 on a Mac. For a reproducible local **structured-output** workflow on Apple Silicon with MIT-licensed parser code, start with **Docling plus retained JSON**, verify the chosen model's weight license and test representative equations and captions, and use MinerU when those checks warrant its extra weight/deployment/license review. Use GROBID for TEI-level citations and metadata, or PyMuPDF4LLM as a CPU text baseline. Preserve the PDF, page/box provenance, and image assets: none of these benchmarks certifies code fences or figure-caption associations ([Li et al., 2025](../../sources/readoc-study/); [OpenDataLab, n.d.](../../sources/omnidocbench-repository/); [NixOS / nixpkgs, n.d.](../../sources/nixpkgs-pdf-parsers/)).
