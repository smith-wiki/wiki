---
title: Decoy logins move the password oracle
summary: Hiding an immediate login rejection helps only while no other response or external fact identifies the genuine session.
---

A real account and a decoy account must differ in what they can ultimately reveal or change. **Inference:** Suppressing the first wrong-password error does not remove the correctness test; it moves it from the login response into content, metadata, timing, or later actions. [Smith's proposed stale AI-generated mail and documents](../../sources/andy-smith-every-password-correct/) could delay a stranger who knows nothing about the account, but neither the post nor the cited precedents establish indistinguishability of a live account. ([Smith, 2026](../../sources/andy-smith-every-password-correct/))

## Where the answer leaks

- **Known facts and freshness:** An attacker who knows a current email, transaction, contact, or timestamp can compare it with the candidate view. Honey encryption explicitly loses its stronger guarantee with useful message side information or correlated messages. A deliberately stale account makes freshness a potential distinguisher. **Inference:** AI-generated fluency does not solve those constraints. ([Juels and Ristenpart, n.d.](../../sources/honey-encryption-juels-ristenpart/))
- **Repeat observations and writes:** A fake account that does not reopen to the same records for the same password, persist edits, or send a message is testable; allowing real effects from a false password is unsafe. [Kamouflage](../../sources/kamouflage-password-management/) updates decoy sets along with the real set so snapshots do not reveal which changed; [VeraCrypt](../../sources/veracrypt-hidden-volume-requirements/) warns that changed blocks and third-party logs defeat local deniability. **Inference:** A live account expands this consistency obligation to every API and integration. ([Bojinov et al., n.d.](../../sources/kamouflage-password-management/); [VeraCrypt project, n.d.](../../sources/veracrypt-hidden-volume-requirements/))
- **Protocol behavior and cost:** HTTP status, response time, redirects, MFA prompts, and recovery paths can distinguish real from decoy before content is inspected. OWASP documents ordinary response and timing discrepancies; NIST requires rate limiting on password guesses, independent of whether the UI displays an error. **Inference:** Calling each fake view a successful login must not silently reset failed-guess counters or grant access to the genuine account; generating a new world for each attacker-supplied string also needs a resource bound. ([OWASP Cheat Sheet Series, n.d.](../../sources/owasp-authentication-cheat-sheet/); [National Institute of Standards and Technology, 2025](../../sources/nist-authenticator-verifier-requirements/))

## Design implication

**Inference:** Treat [password-triggered decoys](../../concepts/password-triggered-decoys/) as deception or intrusion detection, not an authentication replacement. Restrict false-password sessions to isolated synthetic data and capabilities; [object-capability RPC](../../concepts/object-capability-rpc/) makes the authority boundary explicit. Keep genuine access behind normal verifier controls, rate limits, and required factors. Evaluate an attacker with prior knowledge and repeated read/write/API access, not merely a human glancing at one generated page. ([Juels and Rivest, n.d.](../../sources/honeywords-juels-rivest/); [National Institute of Standards and Technology, 2025](../../sources/nist-authenticator-verifier-requirements/))
