---
title: A chunking winner depends on evidence unit and retrieval budget
summary: Passage coverage, document ranking, and top-k retrieval at unequal token volumes can name different chunking winners on the same source material.
---

A chunking method can retrieve a correct **document** while failing to surface the exact **passage** needed to answer or cite a claim. More and longer top-k chunks can increase apparent evidence recall while consuming more context and duplicating overlapping text. Chroma's recursive 400/0 versus 200/0 configurations illustrate the recall/IoU tradeoff; its aggregate appendix also reverses the main table's cluster-versus-LLM recall leader ([Smith and Troynikov, 2024](../../sources/chroma-chunking-study/)). Jina's reported nDCG@10 collapses chunks into parent document rankings, so its measured late-chunking gain alone does not prove passage-level accuracy ([Guenther et al., 2025](../../sources/jina-late-chunking-study/)). Anthropic's failed-recall@20 for contextualized chunks has a different corpus, retrieval depth, and representation intervention ([Ford, 2024](../../sources/anthropic-contextual-retrieval/)).

**Inference:** Choose and publish a gold unit independent of candidate chunk boundaries: an immutable source span or jointly required set of spans. Score its recovery at both top-k and *equal retrieved-token budgets*, charging duplicate overlap and distinguishing parser loss from retrieval failure. Report document-hit, passage-hit, complete-evidence coverage, irrelevant-token cost, and indexing/query cost separately, stratified by [web capture](../../concepts/web-capture-fidelity/), [PDF structure](../../concepts/scientific-pdf-structure-extraction/), and repository content. This operationalizes [evidence-aware retrieval chunking](../../concepts/evidence-aware-retrieval-chunking/) without confusing large-chunk recall with efficient, citable evidence.
