## Claims and evidence

The intended input is one 1,500–2,000 word author essay, published or intended for publication on a blog. It contains original claims, ideas, and explicit or implicit questions. The researcher should treat the essay as one turn, preserve its voice, identify researchable lines, and produce evidence-backed follow-up work useful to later writing.

The request requires selective research rather than checking every sentence. It separates the author’s text from discovered evidence and asks for explicit decision points where scope needs author judgment.

## Limits

The request defines desired behavior; it is not external evidence for claims made inside a future essay. No actual essay was supplied in this turn, so the resulting process is a design to apply and test on the first real text.
