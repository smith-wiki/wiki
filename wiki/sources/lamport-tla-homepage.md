## Overview

Leslie Lamport's personal TLA+ homepage describes TLA+ as a high-level mathematical language for modeling software and systems, especially concurrent and distributed ones, and links to his pages on the language, learning resources, and tools, naming the TLC model checker and the TLAPS proof system. Last modified in October 2025, it says the TLA+ Foundation now stewards the project and that this site is his personal archive rather than the project's current home. It is useful for the creator's framing, while [A High-Level View of TLA+](../lamport-tla-high-level-view/) and [TLA+ Tools](../lamport-tla-tools/) carry the substance.

## Key points

- TLA+ is a high-level mathematical language for modeling software and systems, particularly concurrent and distributed ones, to find fundamental design errors. (Opening paragraph beginning "I am the creator of TLA+")
- The main checking tools are the TLC model checker and the TLAPS proof system. The TLA+ Foundation now stewards the project; Lamport identifies this site as his personal archive. (`The Tools`; opening paragraph beginning "This web page is the home page")
