## Evidence

### `modeling-purpose`

- **Author states:** TLA+ is a high-level mathematical language for modeling software and systems, particularly concurrent and distributed ones, to find fundamental design errors.
- **Locator:** Opening paragraph beginning "I am the creator of TLA+"

### `tooling-and-stewardship`

- **Author states:** The main checking tools are the TLC model checker and the TLAPS proof system. The TLA+ Foundation now stewards the project; Lamport identifies this site as his personal archive.
- **Locator:** `The Tools`; opening paragraph beginning "This web page is the home page"
