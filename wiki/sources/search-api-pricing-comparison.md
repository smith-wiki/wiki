## Claims and evidence

Brave prices Web, LLM Context, Images, News, and Videos at $5 per 1,000 requests and advertises $5 in monthly credit. Its LLM Context endpoint returns extracted, ranked chunks under a configurable token budget; Brave also advertises Zero Data Retention.

Tavily grants 1,000 credits monthly without a credit card. Pay-as-you-go credits cost $0.008; a basic search consumes one credit and an advanced search consumes two. Monthly bundles reduce the unit price.

Exa charges $7 per 1,000 Search requests, including up to ten results. Results above ten cost another $1 per 1,000 results. Exa separately prices page content at $1 per 1,000 pages per content type.

## Limits

These are not interchangeable units. Providers differ in indexes, ranking, result counts, extracted content, latency, privacy terms, and controls. The cited vendors report their own prices and capabilities; this evidence does not independently compare relevance or answer quality.
