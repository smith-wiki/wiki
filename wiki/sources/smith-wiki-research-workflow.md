## Claims and evidence

The contract separates an ignored, immutable `raw/` evidence layer from the replaceable `wiki/` publication layer. Fetched evidence is preserved before synthesis and receives a source note containing its original address, retrieval time, digest, and raw path. Research pages answer one request; entities, concepts, and notes receive only reusable knowledge.

The workflow requires evidence before synthesis, selection rather than indiscriminate collection, and readable provenance links for material factual claims. Private supplied text is not automatically public: publication remains a deliberate repository change.

## Limits

This is a project-specific operating contract, not a general property of OMP. It specifies how Smith Wiki handles evidence after a tool reads or discovers it.
