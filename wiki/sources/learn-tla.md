## Source criticism

This is author-owned instructional material, not the TLA+ Foundation's canonical specification or an independent evaluation. The homepage's endorsement and faster, more confident design claims are not substantiated there. The guide is explicitly under development, and the author's consulting disclosure is relevant when interpreting advocacy.
