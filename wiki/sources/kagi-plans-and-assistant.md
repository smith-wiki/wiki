## Claims and evidence

Starter costs $5 per month plus tax for 300 searches and 300 standard-model AI interactions. Professional costs $10 per month plus tax for unlimited searches, Summarize, and standard-model Assistant. Ultimate costs $25 per month, adds premium Assistant models, and provides up to $25 of monthly model usage; annual individual plans are advertised at 10% off.

Kagi itself recommends starting with Starter and moving to Professional after consistently exhausting 300 searches. Assistant can combine multiple model providers with optional Kagi Search, files, custom instructions, and saved threads. Its value-based allowance includes a 20% margin over underlying token cost.

## Limits

Taxes vary. Search and AI allowances reset according to the subscription terms, and prices or model availability can change. These consumer plans are distinct from API billing.
