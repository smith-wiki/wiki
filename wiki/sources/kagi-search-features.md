## Claims and evidence

Kagi says its ranking down-ranks pages with many ads and trackers and favors independent, ad-free, and noncommercial sources when relevance permits. Users can raise, lower, pin, or block domains so repeated judgments alter later result ordering.

Lenses restrict searches to selected parts of the web. Built-in examples cover forums, programming, academic domains, PDFs, archives, recipes, and Small Web. Small Web is a curated collection of personal sites and independent creators. Quick Answers summarize result sets, while Summarize Page can summarize individual pages and videos.

## Limits

These are Kagi's own product descriptions, not comparative measurements against Google, Bing, or other engines. The value of ranking controls compounds only when a user repeatedly searches and maintains preferences; occasional users may receive less benefit.
