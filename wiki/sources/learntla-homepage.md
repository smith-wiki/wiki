## Evidence

### `design-versus-code`

- **Author distinguishes:** Code defects can violate a design, while design defects persist even in faithfully implemented code. Wayne positions TLA+ as a formal specification language for directly testing designs, supplementing engineering rather than replacing it.
- **Locator:** `Learn TLA+`, paragraphs beginning "Most software flaws" and "TLA+ is a formal specification language"

### `learning-path`

- **Author describes:** The guide divides its material into sequential Core lessons, optional Topics, and worked Examples; it points newcomers to its conceptual overview.
- **Locator:** `About this guide`

## Source criticism

This is an introductory, partly promotional guide by a TLA+ educator and consultant, not an independent evaluation of effectiveness. It identifies itself as incomplete. Its [worked transfer example](../learntla-conceptual-overview/) supplies narrower evidence for the model-checking workflow.
