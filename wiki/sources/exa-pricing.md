## Overview

Exa's own price list covers Search, Contents, Answer, Monitors, Deep Search, and Agent products; the wiki relies only on the standard Search and Contents units. Maintained by the vendor for prospective customers, it is authoritative for the prices advertised at capture time, but it carries no date and can change without notice. Its units do not map one-to-one onto other providers': a Search request includes up to ten results, and ranking, extracted content, and quality differ from [Kagi's API](../kagi-api-pricing/), so price comparisons need that caveat.

## Key points

- Standard Search costs $7 per 1,000 requests and includes up to ten results per request. (Sections "Products", Search; table "Search, Contents, Answer, and Monitors", `/search`)
- Each result beyond the first ten costs another $1 per 1,000 results. (Table "Search, Contents, Answer, and Monitors", `/search`)
- Contents costs $1 per 1,000 pages for each requested content type. (Sections "Products", Contents; "Cost glossary", "Page and content type")
