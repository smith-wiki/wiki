## Overview

Nandakishor Mukkunnoth submitted this preprint on March 30, 2025. Its abstract proposes a specialized reinforcement-learning system for predicting conversion probabilities over successive sales-conversation turns, trained on synthetic GPT-4o-generated data and using Azure OpenAI embeddings. The author reports faster inference and better prediction than an LLM-only approach. The captured abstract and submission record establish chronology and the narrower sales domain, not independent validation of the reported performance or a complete architectural comparison. The later [Laya](../../entities/laya/) family handles request-defined typed questions across domains, while [Jev](../../entities/jev/) is another general-purpose typed-decision product. An earlier sales-conversion model does not by itself establish scientific priority for every feature or that Jev copied Laya.

## Key points

- The initial submission is dated March 30, 2025, before the September 2026 Jev and Laya announcements. (Submission history, v1)
- The abstract describes turn-by-turn sales conversion prediction with reinforcement learning and synthetic GPT-4o training data, not the later general-purpose `choice`/`score`/`noul` interface. (Abstract)
- Reported accuracy, latency, and conversion-rate improvements are author claims in the abstract, not verified by the preserved submission record. (Abstract)
