## Overview

The AWS Pricing Calculator user guide explains how the calculator estimates upfront, monthly, and annual costs for a planned architecture from chosen configurations and entered usage, such as weekly peak traffic. It is undated vendor documentation and is clear that an estimate reflects only the inputs given, excludes taxes, and takes its rates from the [AWS Price List](../aws-price-list/), with service pricing pages prevailing on any discrepancy. It supports treating cost figures as conditional on usage assumptions rather than as predictions of actual charges.

## Key points

- The calculator models solutions before deployment and can estimate upfront, monthly, and annual costs from selected configurations and entered usage patterns, such as weekly peak traffic. (`What is AWS Pricing Calculator?`, opening example)
- Estimated fees are based only on information entered; estimates omit applicable taxes. Calculator prices derive from the AWS Price List API, while service marketing-page prices govern if there is a discrepancy. (`Pricing for AWS Pricing Calculator`)
