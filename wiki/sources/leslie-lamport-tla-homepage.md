## Source criticism

This is a first-party account from TLA+'s creator, but it explicitly ceased to be the project's primary site and may receive only corrections. Its claim that TLA+ eliminates fundamental design errors is not an independent effectiveness study. Use it for Lamport's framing, historical stewardship transition, and resource map rather than current release status or complete Foundation policy.
