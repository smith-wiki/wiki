## Overview

ConvAI Innovations' dashboard privacy policy identifies its operator as Convai Innovations Pvt. Ltd. and provides a Kerala, India, contact address. It is a first-party legal notice for the dashboard, not a model card, company registry, or technical specification for [Laya](../../entities/laya/). The company's [Laya research announcement](../laya-research-announcement/) separately links the ConvAI name and founder to that model. Together with [TypeSafe's terms](../typesafe-terms-of-use/), the documents give concrete evidence that [ConvAI Innovations](../../entities/convai-innovations/) and [TypeSafe AI](../../entities/typesafe-ai/) present themselves under distinct legal names. They do not establish whether the organizations have any undisclosed commercial relationships. The preserved policy states an update date of September 23, 2026.

## Key points

- The policy names Convai Innovations Pvt. Ltd. as the dashboard operator. (Section 1, "Introduction")
- Its contact section lists a Kasaragod, Kerala address and a `convaiinnovations.com` email address. (Section 10, "Contact Us")
- It governs the company's dashboard data handling, not the architecture or training of Laya. (Section 1, "Introduction")
