## Source brief

The official homepage presents [Lean](../../entities/lean/) as both an open-source programming language and a proof assistant for correct, maintainable, formally verified code. Its "Trustworthy" card attributes mathematical proof and software and hardware verification to a minimal trusted kernel; its "Extensible" card says metaprogramming lets users add domain-specific notation and proof automation. ([Lean, 2026](../../sources/lean-lang-homepage/))

Worked examples pair definitions and theorem statements with tactics such as `grind`, while the site routes newcomers directly to installation and learning material. Together, these elements position programming, formal specification, and proof development as parts of one environment rather than separate products. **Inference:** extensibility is central to that combination because proof automation is itself presented as language-level work. ([Lean, 2026](../../sources/lean-lang-homepage/))

**Uncertainty:** this is a mutable, first-party promotional homepage with no publication date or version marker. Its strong "absolute correctness" wording is a project claim, not independent assurance, and the capture does not establish current release status, comparative performance, or how guarantees depend on the trusted computing base. ([Lean, 2026](../../sources/lean-lang-homepage/))
