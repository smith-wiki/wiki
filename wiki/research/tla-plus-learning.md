## Source brief

Leslie Lamport's personal homepage defines [TLA+](../../entities/tla-plus/) as a high-level language for modeling programs and systems, especially concurrent and distributed ones, using simple mathematics. It identifies the Toolbox and VS Code extension as interfaces and TLC and TLAPS as the primary model-checking and proof tools. The page also says stewardship moved to the TLA+ Foundation and describes itself as a legacy personal site. ([Lamport, 2025](../../sources/leslie-lamport-tla-homepage/))

[Learn TLA+](../../sources/learn-tla/) calls TLA+ a formal specification language for testing system designs. Its free guide gives beginners a linear Core, experienced users independent advanced Topics, and both groups worked Examples. Unlike Lamport's resource map, it turns the design rationale into a staged curriculum. ([Wayne, undated](../../sources/learn-tla/))

**Inference:** the sources are complementary: one preserves the creator's project map; the other supplies an author-owned teaching route. **Uncertainty:** both make effectiveness claims without independent evaluation, Learn TLA+ remains a work in progress, and Lamport says his retired personal site may receive only corrections. ([Lamport, 2025](../../sources/leslie-lamport-tla-homepage/); [Wayne, undated](../../sources/learn-tla/))
